Hibinobi Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Our approach

“Hibinobi” (the “App”) handles photos of your child. Everything the App works with stays on your device. There is no developer server. Photos and your child's information are never sent anywhere.

2. What the App stores on your device

DataPurposeWhere
Photos (originals)Keeping, viewing, making videosOn device only
Face position dataAligning faces across photosOn device only
Child's name and birthdayDisplay and age calculationOn device only
Daily notesKeeping and searchingOn device only
App settingsApp behaviourOn device only

3. No data leaves your device

  • Photos, face data, your child's name and birthday, and notes are never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • The App does not ask you to create an account.
  • The App does not sync to iCloud.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera: to take the daily photo.
  • Photos (add only): to save a copy to your photo library.
  • Photos (read): to find photos taken on a chosen day when importing.
  • Notifications: for the daily reminder and backup reminder.
  • Face detection runs on your device using iOS features. No face data is sent anywhere.

5. Storage and backups

Because data is stored on your device, it can be lost if the App is deleted or the device is reset, lost or damaged. Please make regular backups with the App's free ZIP backup and keep them outside the device. You are responsible for where you keep backup files and how you protect them.

6. Purchases

Plus is purchased through Apple. The developer does not receive payment information or your Apple Account. Whether Plus is active is decided on your device from information Apple provides. With Family Sharing, only the Plus entitlement is shared — photos and records are not.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for parents and guardians. Photos stay on the device and are never published. For sharing on social media, a free option to blur the eyes is included. What you share is always your decision.

8. Deleting data

You can delete photos (Trash → delete permanently) and children from within the App. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device. No data remains with the developer.
